Relay: from "delivered" to "invoice-ready"

Product prototypeDashboard UXFront-end build

In freight, a delivered load often can't be billed yet. Finance chases proof of delivery, carrier invoices and rate confirmations across email, spreadsheets, portals and the TMS.

  • Designed an invoice-readiness layer that sits on top of the tools a brokerage already uses.
  • Every delivered load is checked against its billing package; anything missing becomes an exception with an owner and an age.
  • One view of invoice-ready value, blocked value, open exceptions and accessorial reviews.
